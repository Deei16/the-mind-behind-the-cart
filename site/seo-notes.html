SEO implementation notes

This page documents the on-page SEO applied across every page and article on this site, prepared to satisfy the ITP105 — Principles of SEO grading rubric: target keyword, search intent, SEO title, meta description, and URL slug for each page.

Other on-page SEO applied site-wide: one H1 per page, a logical H2/H3 heading hierarchy within each article, descriptive alt text on every image, internal links connecting related articles (main article ↔ dopamine, dopamine ↔ retail therapy, retail therapy ↔ decision fatigue, decision fatigue ↔ main article, endowment effect ↔ main article), at least one credible outbound source link per article, and short, readable URL slugs with no query strings or generic post numbers.
