Consumer psychology, explained plainly

Why do we buy things we don't need?

Shopping isn't always about what we need. Sometimes it's about what we feel. We unpack the dopamine, social pressure, and quiet decision-making shortcuts hiding behind every "add to cart."

Explore the blog Start with the main article

Most purchases don't start with a need. They start with a feeling — boredom on a slow afternoon, a stressful week that needs a small win, or a scroll through a feed full of things other people already own. The Mind Behind the Cart looks at the psychology underneath those moments, so you can notice the pattern before your cart does it for you.
